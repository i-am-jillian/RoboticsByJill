Research

  1. Aug 2026 – Present

    Undergraduate Research Assistant

    DRONES LAB · University at Buffalo

    Contributing to CLEAR (Large-Scale Off-Road Planning with Terrain Abstractions), developing and supporting the autonomy and navigation stack for a Clearpath Jackal UGV. Working with ROS 2 navigation, localization, mapping, and positioning and perception data for off-road operation. Conducting field tests and analyzing navigation and GNSS/RTK data to evaluate performance across challenging terrain.

  2. Oct 2025 – May 2026

    Undergraduate Research Assistant

    Wireless Robotics and Embedded Systems Lab · University at Buffalo

    Researched indoor localization using Wi-Fi Fine Timing Measurement (FTM), IMU sensing, and motion capture. Designed a conceptual pipeline combining wireless ranging and inertial measurements, calibrated IMUs, and collected sensor data to investigate measurement error and localization accuracy. Trained with motion capture systems.

Beyond the lab

Off the clock.

01 / Making

Building for the fun of it.

Even though I build things for my career, I spend much of my free time making small projects as gifts or to make daily life easier. They let me explore circuits, electronics, and other disciplines beyond my curriculum. I made an LED smart clock as a gift, with a Pomodoro timer, guided breathing for calming moments, and little messages throughout the day. I’m also working on an AI digital closet running on an Arduino Q and a Game Boy-style handheld built with a Raspberry Pi.

02 / Puzzles

One more solve.

I enjoy solving puzzles, especially Rubik’s cubes—I have a collection of 15. Working through a solve is a fun way to take a break from engineering while still giving my brain something to figure out.

03 / Reading

A book in the little gaps.

I enjoy reading every day: on the bus, between classes, and in the little pockets of time I find around engineering. Right now, I’m reading Animal Farm by George Orwell and I’m Glad My Mom Died by Jennette McCurdy.
